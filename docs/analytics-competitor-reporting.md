# Analytics Competitor Reporting Research

> Comparison of the ways Clockify and Toggl Track present reports and analytics to users. Researched August 20, 2026.

Both products organize analytics around a similar progression:

**Quick overview → visual breakdown → detailed entries → operational and financial reports**

## Reporting and Analytics Comparison

| Reporting method         | Clockify                                                                                                                         | Toggl Track                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Personal overview        | Personal dashboard showing tracked time, project allocation, billable vs. non-billable time, earnings, and top activities        | Summary report with configurable headline metrics such as total hours, billable percentage, revenue, cost, profit, and daily average  |
| Team overview            | Team dashboard showing total time, current or recent activity, and breakdowns by employee, project, or billability               | Workload report showing time across people, projects, and dates                                                                       |
| Summary report           | Groups time by project, client, user, team, tag, date, week, or month; includes charts and financial totals                      | Groups tracked time by activity dimensions, with headline metrics, charts, and expandable breakdowns                                  |
| Detailed report          | Line-by-line time entries with filtering, editing, bulk editing, auditing, and exporting                                         | Entry-level table with editable columns, sorting, bulk editing, filters, and exports                                                  |
| Weekly or timesheet view | Dedicated weekly report arranged like a timesheet, grouped by user or project                                                    | Workload table uses dates as columns and people, projects, clients, tasks, or tags as expandable rows                                 |
| Project performance      | Estimated versus tracked time, budget progress, costs, revenue, and profit                                                       | Project dashboard forecasts completion from estimates and historical activity; supports time and billing views                        |
| Resource planning        | Assignments report compares scheduled and tracked hours, expected versus realized cost and revenue, availability, and completion | Workload reporting includes utilization and adherence against scheduled hours                                                         |
| Profitability            | Earnings, labor cost, and profit can be included in summary and assignment breakdowns                                            | Dedicated Profitability report with revenue, cost, profit, margin, trend lines, rankings, and a customizable table                    |
| Attendance               | Dedicated attendance report for start and end times, capacity, breaks, absence, time off, overtime, and undertime                | Adherence and utilization reports cover scheduled versus logged and billable hours, but are less like a traditional attendance report |
| Expenses                 | Dedicated expense report with receipts, billable status, approval, and invoicing status                                          | No equivalent dedicated expense report in Toggl Track's core reporting                                                                |
| Data-quality auditing    | Missing time, suspicious entries, users without tracked time, and editable detailed records                                      | Time audits detect short entries, missing projects, missing time, or unusual duration, revenue, or cost                               |
| Custom reports           | Custom filters, multi-level grouping, configurable columns, saved or shared reports, and customized exports                      | Custom reports under **My Reports**, supporting reusable filters, groupings, columns, charts, and sharing                             |
| Delivery                 | PDF, CSV, Excel, live links, scheduled email reports, and invoice creation                                                       | PDF, CSV, Excel, public or internal links, daily, weekly, or monthly email delivery, and invoice creation                             |

## Visual Presentation Patterns

- KPI cards for total time, billable time, revenue, cost, and profit
- Bar and donut charts for project or billability allocation
- Line charts for trends and forecasts
- Ranked bars for top or bottom projects, clients, or people
- Pivot-style workload tables with dates as columns
- Expandable grouped tables for drilling from client to project to task
- Detailed data tables for auditing and editing
- Color-coded thresholds for utilization, adherence, overtime, and budget health
- Persistent date ranges and multi-dimensional filters
- Saved views and scheduled reports for recurring user workflows

## Main Product Distinction

**Clockify separates analytics into more specialized operational reports**, while **Toggl Track is moving toward one flexible, composable reporting workspace**.

For Tickr's redesign, a useful direction would be to combine Clockify's clear report categories with Toggl's configurable KPI-and-chart experience.

## Current Toggl Reporting Transition

Toggl is combining its former Reports, Insights, and Analytics areas into one reporting experience. Saved dashboards are becoming custom reports under **My Reports**.
